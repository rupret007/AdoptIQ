Report Sections

Each Leader report keeps decisions separate from supporting records:

  • Word decision report: concise metrics, canonical charts, account exceptions, and prioritized action plans.
  • Selected scope: an entire team, one team member, or one customer—without mixing unrelated records.
  • Source Data workbook: full supporting Action Plan, Adoption Barrier, Customer Pulse, subscription, and TAC records with metric lineage.
  • Source disclosures: unavailable, partial, or stale inputs are identified rather than silently estimated.
